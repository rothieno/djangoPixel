Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Quick notes about shopping DjangoPixel. This is a demo storefront, so some details are examples rather than live order services.

What can I find in the shop?

The collection is a sample catalog of everyday accessories, home goods, stationery, and useful essentials. Browse by category or search by product name on the shop page.

How do I add an item to my bag?

Choose an item and select “Add to bag.” Your bag is kept in your browser session while you browse. You can update quantities or remove items from the bag page.

How much does shipping cost?

This sample shop shows a $5 shipping charge on orders under ${{ free_shipping_threshold|floatformat:0 }} and free shipping at or above ${{ free_shipping_threshold|floatformat:0 }}. These are example rates, not a live shipping service. See Shipping & returns for details.

Can I place a real order or make a payment?

Not yet. Checkout is a demonstration only: it does not collect payment details, save an order, or arrange delivery. Please don’t enter real personal or payment information.

Can I return or exchange something?

This demo does not process returns or exchanges. A real store return policy and support inbox need to be configured before accepting orders. Read the shipping and returns information.

How can I get in touch?

Visit the Contact page. Its example email address is not a monitored inbox yet.
